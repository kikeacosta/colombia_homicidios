# Historical module (1923-1984 series, WHO and UCDP comparisons)

Scripts, run from the project root, independent of `run_all.R`:

- `20_historic_data_coverage.R`: coverage of the transcribed DANE anuario series (homicides, deaths, population) by year, department and municipality, compared with WHO Mortality Database homicides.
- `21_historic_data_quality_tests.R`: internal consistency tests (total vs male + female, NAs coded as zeros, missing sex splits).
- `22_testing_ucdp.R`: UCDP GED v25.1 conflict deaths for Colombia against WHO homicides.
- `23_census_tables_ocr.R`: builds `data_input/historical/census_tables/` from the 1938, 1951, 1964 and 1973 census summary PDFs: tesseract grid OCR of the 1951 Cuadro 10 (department × 5-year age × sex, 1938 and 1951) and of the 1964 Cuadros 7 and 9 (department × 5-year age × sex × zone, and national single ages × sex × zone; five passes) and of the 1964 Cuadro 14 (place of birth × place of residence × sex × broad age group, 420 blocks of 12 cells tied by seven identities; three passes), reconciled by accounting identities plus manual corrections, and visual transcriptions of the 1938 and 1973 national single-age tables, of the 1973 coverage-adjusted population by department and zone, and of the 1973 tables of women aged 15+ by children ever born and surviving (Cuadro 21 by age group and zone, Cuadro 21A by department and zone). Runs from the versioned `ocr_support/` files; `run_ocr <- TRUE` repeats the OCR from the PDF. Stops if any identity fails. See `data_input/historical/census_tables/README.md`.
- `24_dane_vitales_1958_1960.R`: builds `data_input/historical/dane_vitales_1958_1960_departamental.csv` and `_municipal.csv` from the DANE publication "Estadística vital: matrimonios, nacimientos (bautizos) y defunciones por municipios, 1958 a 1960" (`LD_558_1958_1960_EJ_2.pdf`, not versioned). The municipal tables (marriages; baptisms, deaths under one year and deaths at all ages, each H/M/T) are read cell by cell from the PDF text layer, two tesseract passes and the earlier transcription of the death columns; the combination that satisfies H + M = T is kept, unit-columns are checked against their TOTAL row and the TOTAL rows against the departmental table, and what remains is read by eye and recorded in `data_input/historical/vitales_1958_1960_support/` (`corrections.csv`, `additions.csv`, `rows_read.csv`, `headers.csv`, `codes.csv`). 674 of 700 unit-columns verify; the 26 that do not are inconsistencies of the printed source or columns with "..." cells, listed in `verification_unit_columns.csv` and in the `flag` column. The first run takes ~40 min (OCR, cached in `data_inter/`), later runs ~1 min.
- `25_dane_defunciones_1970_1978.R`: builds the six `data_input/historical/dane_defunciones_1970_1978_cuadro*.csv` files from DANE/UNICEF, "Registro de defunciones en Colombia, 1970-1978" (`LD_8789_1970_1978_EJ_3.pdf`, not versioned): Cuadro 1 (national deaths 1915-1984), Cuadro 2 (deaths by sex and sección, 1970-1984), Cuadro 3 (national, by zone, sex and age, 1970-1978), Cuadro 6 (national, by age, sex and cause of ICD-8 list A, 1973-1978), Cuadro 10 (under five by detailed age and sex, by sección) and Cuadro 11 (by sex and age, national and by sección, 1970-1978). Numbers are placed in columns by the word boxes of the PDF text layer (cached in `data_inter/`), run-together rows of Cuadro 6 are split, and all 94,092 cells are tied by 47,874 accounting identities within and between tables; a cell is corrected only when none of its identities holds and two agree on the value (146 cells, each compared by eye with a crop of the page: 126 text-layer misreadings, 20 misprints of the print). The 112 identities that still fail are inconsistencies of the printed source, listed in `dane_defunciones_1970_1978_support/source_inconsistencies.csv`; the script stops if the failing set differs from that list. Runs in ~1 min (~2 the first time).
- `26_dane_defunciones_1968_1969.R`: builds `data_input/historical/dane_defunciones_1968_1969_secciones_sexo_edad.csv` from the DANE Anuario de Estadísticas Vitales 1968-1969 (`LD_70106_1968_1969_EJ_3.pdf`, not versioned): Cuadro 30 (1968, pp. 56-63) and Cuadro 76 (1969, pp. 143-153), deaths by sex and age group for 31 secciones (22 departments, Bogotá D.E., 4 intendencias and 4 comisarías printed one by one) and the national total; columns and age labels as in `dane_defunciones_1970_1978_cuadro11_secciones_edad.csv`. Blocks (2 to 4 per page) are found from their TOTAL line in the word boxes of the text layer (cached in `data_inter/`), rows are placed by vertical distance and columns by clusters of right edges; 4,992 cells are tied by 2,012 identities (total = men + women, age rows = TOTAL row, secciones = TOTAL NACIONAL, which equals Cuadro 1 of 1970-1978). Cells read by eye on 300-dpi crops go in `dane_defunciones_1968_1969_support/corrections.csv` (24); the identities then correct a cell only when two agree (106, each checked by eye on a contact sheet of crops: 103 text-layer misreadings, 3 misprints of the print in `misprints_in_source.csv`). The 12 identities still failing are inconsistencies of the printed source (`source_inconsistencies.csv`: Bolívar 1968 1-6 days and La Guajira 1968 85+, men and women swapped; Guainía 1969); the script stops if the failing set changes. Cuadros 29/29A and 75 (national deaths by cause, sex and age, 150 groups) are not extracted: the age columns are split over five page sets per year with multi-line cause names and garbled cells, so each cause needs its own reconstruction (Cuadro 6 of 1970-1978 took most of script 25). `crop_block_1968_1969.R` and `crop_cells_1968_1969.R` are the helpers that make the 300-dpi crops (run after the script; they read `data_inter/`). Runs in ~10 s from the cached boxes (~1 min the first time).
- `27_censo_1973_muestra_avance.R`: builds `data_input/historical/census_tables/census_1973_muestra_single_age_sex_dept.csv` (population by sex and single age, 0-84 and 85+) and `census_1973_muestra_age5_sex_dept.csv` (by 5-year age group, the national block also by zone) for the 22 departments and Bogotá D.E. of the 1973 census, from DANE, "La población en Colombia 1973. XIV Censo Nacional de Población y III de Vivienda, muestra de avance" (`data_input/dane_pdfs/censos/1973/LD_1007_1973_EJ_4.pdf`, not versioned; Cuadro 1 and Cuadro 2 of each of the 23 sections, pp. 72 + 11 k and 73 + 11 k, and the 23 sections added, pp. 40-41). **Sample-expanded figures** (1 household in 25, ×25, ×28 in Bogotá; no territories, armed forces, collective households or indigenous population), not the final count: 19.7 million persons against 20.67 million censused. The text layer of the PDF is read by word boxes (cached in `data_inter/`), and the 7,824 cells are tied by 4,579 identities (total = men + women, ages = TOTAL, Cuadro 1 = Cuadro 2, groups = single ages, 23 sections = national, every cell a multiple of 25 or 28); 174 cells of the text layer were wrong or empty and are corrected where two or more identities agree and a 300 dpi crop confirms (`ocr_support/corrections_1973_muestra.csv`, `find_corrections <- TRUE` proposes them); the only failing identities, four, come from one misprint of the print (Boyacá, age 56, total) and are listed in `ocr_support/source_inconsistencies_1973_muestra.csv`; the script stops if that set changes. It also compares the national single ages with `census1973_single_age_sex.csv` (`ocr_support/compare_1973_census_vs_muestra_single_age.csv`: the muestra is 95.5 % of the census, 89.6 % at age 0 and about 93-97 % at the other ages). Runs in ~10 s.
- `31a_extract_conciliacion_small_tables.R`, `31b_extract_conciliacion_annex1_lifetables.R`, `31c_extract_conciliacion_annex3_lifetables.R`: build the `data_input/historical/dane_conciliacion_1985_2005_*.csv` files from DANE, "Conciliación censal 1985-2005. Estimación de la mortalidad" (`data_input/dane_pdfs/censos/5Mortalidad85_05.pdf`): tables 3.3, 3.13, 3.14 and Annex 2 (31a), Annex 1 life tables for 1985, 1993 and 2005 (31b) and Annex 3 quinquennial life tables (31c), with identity checks and a corrections log in `dane_conciliacion_1985_2005_support/`.

Inputs:

- `data_input/historical/census_tables/`: the nine census csvs above, the two `census_1973_muestra_*` csvs of 27 and `ocr_support/` (OCR grids of every pass, `corrections_1951.csv`, `corrections_1964_rows.csv`, `corrections_1964_cuadro14.csv`, `transcription_*.csv`, and for 27 `corrections_1973_muestra.csv`, `source_inconsistencies_1973_muestra.csv` and the comparison with the national table). The PDFs `LD_771_1938_V_1.pdf`, `LD_771_1951.pdf`, `LD_771_1964.pdf` and `LB_771_1973.pdf` are not versioned (35 MB).
- `data_input/historical/dane_vitales_1958_1960_departamental.csv`, `_municipal.csv`: output of 24 (see above). The municipal file has one row per municipality and year with `cod_munic` (DIVIPOLA; 16 rows without code: the six municipalities annexed to Bogotá in 1954 and still listed in 1960, suppressed municipalities and two Amazonas corregimientos), the ten value columns, the source of each column group, `nodata_in_source` for cells printed "..." and `flag` for units whose columns do not add up. Against `col_gap_analysis_..._municipal_deaths_clean_v03.csv` the death totals agree in all but five municipality-years, all errors of the earlier transcription or misprints of the source (Risaralda 1958 390 → 290; San Sebastián de Buenavista 1959, columns shifted; Pinchote 1959 50 → 59; La Vega (Cauca) 1960 331 → 332; Istmina 1959, where the transcription filed San José del Palmar's 56 deaths).
- `data_input/historical/dane_defunciones_1970_1978_cuadro*.csv`: output of 25 (see above). Long files with `male`, `female`, `total`; keys `year`, `unit` and `cod_dpto` (22 departments, `Bogotá D.E.`, `Intendencias y comisarías` pooled, `Total nacional`), `age` (`all`, `under 1 day`, `1-6 days`, `7-29 days`, `1-5 months`, `6-11 months`, `12-23 months`, `2-4`, `5-9` ... `80-84`, `85+`, `not stated`; Cuadro 10 adds the sub-totals `under 5`, `under 1`, `1-4`), `zone` in Cuadro 3 and `cause_code`/`cause_name` in Cuadro 6 (`TOTAL` plus the list A codes with deaths; 138-146 accidents, 147 suicide, 148 homicide and legal intervention, 149 undetermined intent; 150, war, has no deaths). Values are the printed ones except the 20 misprints the identities settle (`misprints_in_source.csv`). Known defects left as printed: the Intendencias y comisarías block of Cuadro 11 in 1973, 1976 and 1977 distributes its (correct) total by age differently from Cuadro 10 and from the national row; in Cuadro 6, 1977, nine causes have a Total general above their age detail (129 deaths). Cuadro 2 carries the printed marks: `*` Cauca, Nariño and Valle (collection and processing problems; e.g. 452, 46 and 900 deaths in 1970), `**` Antioquia, Atlántico and Córdoba (coverage). The departmental totals by sex agree with `col_gap_analysis_..._department_deaths_clean_v06.csv` in all 375 unit-years of 1970-1984.
- `data_input/historical/col_gap_analysis_1920_1985_*.csv`: series transcribed from DANE anuarios estadísticos and boletines (versioned, CC-BY 4.0). Common columns `department`/`municipality`, `year`, `total`, `male`, `female`, `data_available`, `sex_disagg_available`, plus quality notes (`known_data_problem`, or `data_flag_dane_*` / `data_flag_collaborator_*` and `source_new` in the departmental deaths file).
  - `department_deaths_clean_v06.csv` (`;`-separated), 1915-1984, with two territorial variants: `_territories_aggregated` (25 units, intendencias and comisarías summed into their department of origin; the default in 20 and 21) and `_territories_split` (33 units). The aggregated sum matches the national file except in 1926, 1931, 1932, 1934 and 1956-1961 (Bogotá counted as its own row and inside Cundinamarca); 21 prints those years.
  - `department_homicides_clean_v02.csv` (`,`), 1923-1965. There is no national homicide file: 20 and 21 sum the departments with data in each year.
  - `department_population_clean_v03.csv` (`;`), censuses 1905-1973.
  - `municipal_deaths_clean_v03.csv` and `municipal_population_clean_v03.csv` (`,`), 1924-1960 and censuses 1938-1964.
  - `national_deaths_clean_v03.csv` and `national_population_clean_v02.csv` (`;`), 1923-1984 and 1905-1985. The national deaths file is also the anuario total used by `code/22_ddm_census_national.R`.
- `data_input/historical/archive_v01_v02/`: the previous transcription (October 2026), kept for reference and read by no script. Against it, the new files add 1915-1922, sex splits for 1923-1949, source and quality notes per cell, and fix transcription errors (e.g. Valle del Cauca 1979, 4,818 → 14,818; Santander 1947, 6,091 → 11,499).
- `data_inter/who_homicide_colombia.rds`, `data_inter/who_all_cause_colombia.rds`: extracts of the WHO Mortality Database. **No script in this repository produces them.** Convert them to CSV under `data_input/reference/who/` with a source note before publishing.
- `data_input/ucdp/GEDEvent_v25_1.csv`, `GEDEvent_v25_01_25_12.csv`: UCDP GED, not versioned (270 MB). Download from https://ucdp.uu.se/downloads/ and cite UCDP GED v25.1.

The source inventory of variables, years and known gaps is `documents/datos_mortalidad_colombia_03_2026.xlsx`.
