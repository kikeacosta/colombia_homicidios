# Figures

One folder per analysis. PDFs (large multi-page maps) are written by the
scripts but not versioned.

| folder | script | content |
|---|---|---|
| `01_descriptives/` | 04, 05 | national, department and municipal sex ratios and homicide rates |
| `02_model/` | 06, 07, 08 | excess deaths, model predictions, validation maps |
| `03_cause_decomposition/` | 09 | cause-specific contributions to the all-cause sex ratio |
| `04_completeness/` | 10, 14, 18, 23, 27, 28, 33, 34, 35, 36 | registration completeness: growth balance, DANE life tables, DDM, WPP vs MSE, life tables compared, Adair-Lopez empirical completeness (national, departmental, 2014 validation), our series against WPP 2024, age decomposition of the e0 gap, completeness methods compared with census correction factors |
| `05_reconstruction/` | 37, 38, 39 | national cohort-component reconstruction: WPP net migration by age and sex, mortality scenarios against the censuses, Bayesian estimation, population given our mortality |
| `06_components/` | 40, 41, 42, 43, 44, 45, 46, 47 | registered deaths in three components, UN IGME under-five mortality, the adjusted series by component (natural model, old-age model, against WPP, variants), Brass child mortality from the 1973 census, NRC 1982 life tables against the series, the Brass arbitration of the NRC gap (under five against 5+), the adjusted rates against WPP 2024 (Lexis surface of the ratio, rates by age in selected years), and the crisis component (police and La Violencia floors of the violent deaths, Armero 1985) |
| `07_departments/` | 50, 51 | the national adjustment run by department 1985-2023: e0 by department (adjusted, registered, DANE), the completeness applied (annual, smoothed, calibrated), the composition in 2000, the sum of the departments against the national series, and the MSE conflict deaths allocated to departments against the EEVV-proportional key |
| `05_underregistration_tests/` | 12, 14, 16 | tests of the under-registration hypothesis, summary chart |
| `historical/` | 20, 22 | 1923-1984 series, UCDP and WHO comparison |
| `experimental/` | model_spatial | spatial model variant |
| `inventory/` | 32 | data availability by year, geographic level and disaggregation (tile chart for the inventory documents) |
